# Project Report: Vendor and Sales Performance Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of vendor and sales performance based on the provided dataset. The primary goal was to identify key trends, uncover actionable insights, and provide data-driven recommendations to optimize procurement, sales strategies, and overall profitability. The analysis revealed a strong dependency on a few key vendors, significant cost savings from bulk purchasing, and a notable difference in profit margins between high and low-performing vendors.

## 2. Key Findings

### 2.1. Vendor Dependency and Concentration
- **High Concentration:** The top 10 vendors account for approximately 66% of the total purchase dollars, with DIAGEO NORTH AMERICA INC being the largest single contributor (16.3%).
- **Supply Chain Risk:** This high level of dependency on a small number of vendors poses a significant risk to the supply chain. Any disruption from these key suppliers could have a major impact on business operations.
- **Underutilized Vendors:** A large number of vendors contribute a very small percentage of the total procurement, suggesting they are underutilized or less competitive.

### 2.2. Sales and Profitability Insights
- **Top Performers:** DIAGEO NORTH AMERICA INC, MARTIGNETTI COMPANIES, and PERNOD RICARD USA are the top 3 vendors by sales performance, contributing significantly to the total revenue.
- **Brand Performance:** "Jack Daniels No 7 Black," "Tito's Handmade Vodka," and "Grey Goose Vodka" are the top-selling brands.
- **Profit Margin Discrepancy:** There is a statistically significant difference in profit margins between top-performing and low-performing vendors. Low-performing vendors, despite lower sales, tend to have higher profit margins (40.5% - 42.6% CI) compared to top-performing vendors (30.7% - 31.6% CI). This suggests different pricing strategies or cost structures.
- **Promotional Opportunities:** Several brands were identified with low sales but high profit margins. These brands are prime candidates for targeted promotional activities or pricing adjustments to boost sales volume without significantly compromising profitability.

### 2.3. Inventory and Procurement Analysis
- **Bulk Purchasing Benefits:** A clear and significant cost advantage was observed with bulk purchasing. The average unit purchase price for large orders is approximately 72% lower than for small orders. This highlights the effectiveness of the current bulk pricing strategy.
- **Slow-Moving Inventory:** Several vendors were identified with low inventory turnover rates (StockTurnover < 1), indicating the presence of slow-moving or excess stock. This ties up capital and increases holding costs.
- **Capital Locked in Unsold Inventory:** A total of $2.71 million is locked in unsold inventory. DIAGEO NORTH AMERICA INC, JIM BEAM BRANDS COMPANY, and PERNOD RICARD USA are the top 3 vendors with the highest value of unsold inventory.

## 3. Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

### 3.1. Diversify the Vendor Base
- **Onboard New Suppliers:** Actively seek and onboard new, competitive vendors to reduce dependency on the top 10 suppliers. This will mitigate supply chain risks and can lead to better pricing and terms.
- **Consolidate Smaller Vendors:** Evaluate the performance of the numerous small vendors. Consolidate purchasing with the most promising of these to improve efficiency and potentially negotiate better terms.

### 3.2. Optimize Sales and Marketing Strategies
- **Promote High-Margin, Low-Sales Brands:** Launch targeted marketing campaigns and promotions for brands identified as having high profit margins but low sales. This can include in-store promotions, discounts, or bundling with popular products.
- **Learn from Low-Performing Vendors:** Investigate the pricing and cost structures of low-performing vendors with high profit margins. Their strategies might offer insights that can be applied to other product lines.

### 3.3. Enhance Inventory Management
- **Address Slow-Moving Stock:** For vendors with low inventory turnover, develop strategies to clear excess stock. This could include clearance sales, promotional bundles, or negotiating buy-back agreements with vendors.
- **Optimize Ordering for Bulk Discounts:** Encourage smaller vendors to increase their order sizes by highlighting the significant cost savings available through bulk purchasing.

### 3.4. Further Analysis
- **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze sales data by customer segments to understand purchasing behavior and tailor marketing efforts more effectively.
- **Geographic Analysis:** If location data is available, perform a geographic analysis of sales to identify regional trends and opportunities.
- **Seasonality Analysis:** Investigate sales data for seasonal patterns to optimize inventory and marketing efforts throughout the year.

By implementing these recommendations, the business can build a more resilient supply chain, optimize its sales and pricing strategies, and improve overall profitability.
